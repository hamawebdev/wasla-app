# Wasla — Product Overview

> **Wasla** (Arabic: *وصلة*, "the connection / the link") is a mobile marketplace that connects everyday customers with trusted local service providers — small home-based businesses, craftspeople, and independent professionals — in their own city.

---

## What is Wasla?

Wasla is a two-sided mobile application (iOS and Android) for the Algerian local-services market. It brings small, often home-based businesses online and makes them easy to discover, contact, book, pay, and review — all from a single app.

The app is built around two complementary experiences that share one product:

- **A customer app** — for people looking to find and book a service near them.
- **A provider app** — for service owners who want to showcase their work, receive requests, manage clients, and grow their business.

A person chooses their role when they first sign in, and the app adapts its entire navigation, home screen, and feature set to that role.

The product is fully localized for the region:

- **Languages:** Arabic (primary, right-to-left), English, and French.
- **Currency:** Algerian Dinar (DZD).
- **Geography:** Algerian wilayas (provinces) and cities — e.g. Algiers, Oran, Constantine, Annaba.
- **Local payment methods:** Cash on delivery, Gold Card (bank card), and BaridiMob (mobile wallet).

## Who is it for?

### Customers
People who need a local service and want a trusted, convenient way to find one — for example:

- Someone who needs a dress tailored, a cake for a celebration, or bridal makeup.
- A household that needs cleaning, home maintenance, or a phone repaired.
- A family looking for private tutoring, a photographer, event planning, or delivery help.

### Service providers
Small business owners and skilled individuals who offer services locally and want more customers without building their own website or app — for example:

- A seamstress, baker, beautician, or home-cleaning professional.
- A phone-repair technician, event planner, tutor, photographer, or digital marketer.

### Stakeholders & internal teams
Product managers, business teams, and customer-support staff who need to understand what the app offers, how users move through it, and where the boundaries of each feature lie.

## Core value proposition

| For customers | For providers |
|---|---|
| Discover trusted, nearby services in minutes | Get an online storefront with zero setup cost |
| Book appointments with a guided, step-by-step flow | Receive and manage booking requests in one place |
| Communicate safely in-app without sharing a personal phone number | Chat with clients and confirm details before sharing contacts |
| Compare providers by rating, distance, and price | Build reputation through ratings and reviews |
| Earn loyalty points and rewards for activity | Promote services to appear at the top of search |
| Pay with familiar local methods | Track earnings and withdraw via a built-in wallet |

The recurring themes across the product are **trust** (verified providers, ratings, reviews), **safety** (in-app chat that protects personal numbers), **locality** (distance, city, and map-based discovery), and **engagement** (loyalty points, badges, and promotions).

---

## Main benefits

- **Find services near you** — search, filter, and browse by category or on a map, sorted by distance, price, or rating.
- **Book with confidence** — a clear, multi-step booking flow with date/time selection, address, payment method, and a transparent price breakdown.
- **Communicate safely** — built-in messaging keeps personal phone numbers private until a booking is confirmed.
- **Stay informed** — live booking tracking, status updates, and notifications keep customers in the loop from request to completion.
- **Be rewarded** — customers earn points and badges; providers can spend points to promote their store.
- **Manage money in one place** — providers see earnings, top up, and withdraw through an in-app wallet.

## User roles

| Role | Description | Primary goals |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer** | A person searching for and booking services. | Discover services, book appointments, chat, track, pay, review, and earn loyalty rewards. |
| **Service Provider** | A business owner offering one or more services. | Set up a store, list services, receive and accept requests, manage clients, chat, get paid, and promote. |

A user's role is selected at sign-in and determines which "home" they land on and which navigation tabs they see. Providers go through an additional one-time **store setup** before reaching their dashboard.

## Core workflows

### Customer journey (high level)
1. **Onboarding** — a short welcome introduces the app's value.
2. **Sign in** — choose a role and verify a phone number with a one-time code.
3. **Set location** — allow location access or pick a city manually.
4. **Discover** — browse the home feed, search, filter, or explore a map.
5. **Choose** — open a service or provider store to see details, photos, and reviews.
6. **Book** — pick a date and time, choose an address and payment method, apply a promo code, and confirm.
7. **Communicate & track** — chat with the provider and follow the booking's live status.
8. **Complete & review** — rate the experience and earn loyalty points.

### Provider journey (high level)
1. **Sign in & choose the provider role.**
2. **Set up the store** — add a business name, category, work photos, and a service area.
3. **List services** — create services with descriptions, prices, photos, and availability.
4. **Receive requests** — see new booking requests on the dashboard.
5. **Accept or decline** — accepting confirms the booking and opens a chat with the client.
6. **Manage clients** — view client profiles and booking history.
7. **Get paid & grow** — track earnings in the wallet and promote services to reach more customers.

## Summary of major capabilities

### Customer capabilities
- Onboarding, role selection, and phone-based sign-in
- Location permission and manual city selection
- Home feed with featured and nearby services
- Search with filters (category, price, rating, distance, availability) and sorting
- Category browsing
- Service detail pages with photo galleries, reviews, and provider info
- Provider store pages (About / Services / Reviews)
- Favorites (saved services)
- Map-based service discovery
- Multi-step booking with promo codes and a transparent price breakdown
- Bookings list with Current / Completed / Cancelled tabs
- Live booking tracking with call and chat shortcuts
- Booking cancellation with reasons
- In-app chat with safety guidance, location sharing, and appointment reminders
- Ratings and reviews
- Loyalty program: points, tiers, badges, and a rewards catalogue
- Profile management, saved addresses, notifications, language, and theme

### Provider capabilities
- Provider role sign-in and a guided store setup
- Dashboard with key stats (new requests, rating, monthly earnings) and weekly availability
- Service management (create, list, mark active/inactive)
- Booking request management (New / Current / Completed) with accept and decline
- Client directory and individual client profiles with history
- In-app chat with customers
- Wallet with balance, top-up, withdrawal, and transaction history
- Store promotion (featured placement) paid with loyalty points or directly
- Customer-preview of their own store
- Profile and settings management

### Shared capabilities
- Notifications center
- Help center with searchable articles, categories, and support contact
- Profile editing
- Saved addresses
- App settings (theme, language, legal links, support)
