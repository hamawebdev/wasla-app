## Getting started

Wasla is a mobile app for finding and booking local services in Algeria — or, if you're a business owner, for receiving and managing bookings. It works in **Arabic, English, and French**, supports **light and dark themes**, and uses the **Algerian Dinar (DZD)**.

When you open Wasla for the first time, you'll see a short **welcome introduction** explaining what the app does:

1. Discover trusted services near you.
2. Connect safely in-app — no need to share your personal phone number.
3. Earn points and ratings as you use the app.

Swipe through the slides, then tap **Start now**, or choose **I already have an account**.

---

## Creating an account and signing in

1. **Choose how you'll use Wasla.** Select either:
   - **I'm looking for a service** (Customer), or
   - **I provide services** (Provider).
2. **Register.** Enter your full name and your phone number (with the +213 Algerian prefix). Email is optional. You can also continue with Google or Facebook.
3. **Verify your phone.** Enter the six-digit code sent to your phone. If it doesn't arrive, wait for the timer and tap **Resend**.
4. **You're in.**
   - **Customers** go straight to the home screen.
   - **Providers** continue to a quick store setup (see [Setting up your store](#setting-up-your-store)).

> **Demo mode:** In the current build you may instead see an **account picker** that lets you sign in instantly with a ready-made demo customer or provider account, and any six-digit verification code is accepted.

## Setting your location

To show you the closest services, Wasla asks for your location:

- Tap **Allow location** to use your device's GPS, **or**
- Tap **Choose manually** to pick your city/wilaya from a list (you can search for a neighborhood or city), then **Confirm**.

You can browse without sharing GPS — just set your city manually.

## Navigation overview

Wasla uses a **bottom tab bar** that adapts to your role.

**Customer tabs:**
| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Featured and nearby services, search, and category filters |
| **Search** | Detailed search with filters and sorting |
| **Bookings** | Your current, completed, and cancelled bookings |
| **Chats** | Conversations with providers |
| **Account** | Profile, settings, and shortcuts |

Additional customer screens (Favorites, Points & Rewards, Map) are reached from the home screen and your account.

**Provider tabs:**
| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Stats, availability, and latest bookings |
| **Services** | Your service listings |
| **Add (＋)** | Quick action to add a service or promotion |
| **Chats** | Conversations with clients |
| **Account** | Profile, wallet, settings, and shortcuts |

## Customer guide

### Finding a service

**From the Home tab:**
- Read your greeting and use the **search bar** at the top.
- Tap a **category** to filter (e.g. Sewing & Tailoring, Beauty & Makeup, Home Cleaning).
- Scroll the **Featured for you** carousel and the **Services near you** list.
- Tap the **Map / List** toggle to view services on a map.
- Tap the **bell** to open notifications.

**From the Search tab:**
1. Type what you're looking for.
2. Open **Filters** to narrow by category, price range, rating, distance, or availability.
3. Choose a **Sort** order: Nearest, Price (low/high), or Highest rated.
4. Tap a result to open it. Use **Clear** to reset active filters.

**Browsing a category:** Tapping a category shows all its services with a result count.

### Viewing a service or provider

**Service page** shows:
- A photo gallery, title, rating, review count, distance, and city.
- The provider, an expandable description, and details: duration, service type, and payment method.
- The price (some are shown as "starting from").
- Actions: **Save** (heart), **Share**, **Chat**, **View store**, **Write a review**, and **Book now**.

**Provider store** shows tabs for **About**, **Services**, and **Reviews**, plus the provider's rating, response rate, and an "in demand" indicator. You can **Book now** or **Contact** from here.

### Booking a service

1. On a service page, tap **Book now**.
2. **Step 1 — Date & time:** Pick an available date on the calendar and choose a time slot, then continue. (Past dates are disabled.)
3. **Step 2 — Details & payment:**
   - Choose a **service address** (a saved one or **+ Add new address**).
   - Add any **additional notes**.
   - Pick a **payment method**: Cash, Gold Card, or BaridiMob.
   - Optionally enter a **promo code** (e.g. a welcome discount) and tap apply.
   - Review the **price details**: service cost, platform fee, any discount, and the grand total.
   - Tap **Confirm booking**.
4. **Confirmation:** You'll see that your request was sent and is **awaiting confirmation**. From here you can **Track provider**, **Message provider**, **Explore services**, or go **Back to home**.

### Tracking and managing bookings

Open the **Bookings** tab to see your bookings in three tabs:
- **Current** — pending or confirmed bookings.
- **Completed** — finished bookings.
- **Cancelled** — cancelled bookings.

For an active booking, open **Track service** to follow the provider's progress through **Accepted → On the way → Arrived**, see them on a map, and **Call** or **Chat** directly.

### Cancelling a booking

1. From a booking, choose **Cancel booking**.
2. Select a reason: plans changed, found an alternative, price too high, provider unavailable, or other.
3. Add optional notes and **Confirm cancellation**.

### Messaging a provider

- Open the **Chats** tab to see your conversations. Filter by **All** or **Bookings**.
- Inside a chat you can send messages, **send your location**, and send an **appointment reminder**.
- A safety banner reminds you to **avoid sharing your phone number before the booking is confirmed**. You can also **report** a conversation.

### Leaving a review

1. After a service, open it and tap **Write a review** (or use the review action on the booking).
2. Select a **star rating**, add optional **photos**, and write **details**.
3. Tap **Submit review**. You'll earn loyalty points for reviewing.

### Favorites

- Tap the **heart** on any service to save it.
- Open **Favorites** to see everything you've saved, ready to compare or book later.

### Points and rewards

Open **Points & Rewards** to:
- See your **points balance** and **current tier** (e.g. Silver), with progress toward the next tier (e.g. Gold).
- View **achievements/badges** — unlock them by completing bookings, writing reviews, trying new categories, and more.
- Browse the **rewards catalogue** and **redeem** points for benefits like discounts or a mobile top-up.
- Check your **points history** of everything earned and spent.

## Provider guide

### Setting up your store

After choosing the provider role and verifying your phone, complete the three-step setup:
1. **Project info** — enter your business name and pick your main category.
2. **Work photos** — add up to five photos of your work.
3. **Service area** — set the radius (in km) around your location that you serve.

Tap **Finish** to open your dashboard.

### Your dashboard

The **Dashboard** tab shows:
- **Stats:** new bookings, overall rating, and monthly earnings.
- **This week's availability** strip.
- A **View new requests** shortcut.
- **Latest bookings** — tap one to open the client.

### Adding and managing services

- Open the **Services** tab to see your listings, each marked **Active** or **Inactive**.
- Tap **+ Add service** (or the **＋** tab) and complete the four steps:
  1. **Name & category**
  2. **Description & price** — set a fixed price or a "starting from" price.
  3. **Work photos** — up to five.
  4. **Availability hours**
- On success, your service will start appearing to customers.

### Handling booking requests

Open **Requests / Clients** to see bookings in three tabs: **New**, **Current**, and **Completed**.
- For a **New** request, tap **Accept** or **Reject**.
- **Accepting** confirms the booking and opens a **chat** with the client.
- **Rejecting** cancels the request.

### Managing clients

Open a **client profile** to see their total and completed bookings, their booking history, and a way to **contact** them.

### Your wallet

Open the **Wallet** to:
- See your **available balance**.
- **Top up** your balance or **withdraw earnings**.
- Review your **transaction history** (income, expense, top-up, withdrawal) with statuses.

> Top-up and withdrawal are demonstrated with sample data in the current build.

### Promoting your services

Open **Promote your services** to feature your store. Promotion:
- Puts your services at the **top of search results**.
- Adds a **Featured** badge.
- Notifies nearby customers.
- Lasts **7 days**.

Pay with **loyalty points** (e.g. 500 points) or **buy directly** through the payment gateway.

### Previewing your store

Use **Customer Preview** to see your store exactly as customers do — a banner confirms you're in preview mode. Check your About, Services, and Reviews before going live.

## Shared features

### Notifications

Open **Notifications** (the bell, or from your account) to see booking and message alerts grouped by **Today**, **Yesterday**, and **This Week**. Tap **Mark all as read** to clear them.

### Addresses

Open **My Addresses** to manage where services are delivered:
- **Add** an address with a type (Home, Work, Other), city, detailed address, and optional notes (e.g. floor and apartment).
- **Set a default** address and **delete** ones you no longer need.

### Profile and settings

Open the **Account** tab to:
- See your name, role, and shortcuts.
- **Edit profile** — update your name, email, and bio (your verified phone number is changed via support; photo upload is coming in a future update).
- Toggle **notifications** and **dark mode**, and change **language** (Arabic, English, French).
- Open **Help center** or **Log out**.

App **Settings** also offer theme (light/dark/system), language, legal links (privacy policy, terms), support, share, and rate options, plus the app version.

### Help center

Open the **Help Center** to:
- **Search** for help.
- Browse categories: **Account, Bookings, Payment, Complaints**.
- Read **popular articles** and full articles.
- If you can't find an answer, use **Live Chat** or **Call** support.

## Common workflows

**Book a service (customer):**
> Home/Search → open a service → **Book now** → pick date & time → choose address, payment, and promo code → **Confirm** → track and chat.

**Find the best-rated provider nearby (customer):**
> Search → set filters (rating, distance) → Sort by **Highest rated** → open the provider's store → **Book now**.

**Redeem a reward (customer):**
> Points & Rewards → browse the rewards catalogue → **Redeem** with points.

**Accept a job (provider):**
> Dashboard → **View new requests** → open a request → **Accept** → chat with the client to confirm details.

**List a new service (provider):**
> Services → **+ Add service** → name & category → description & price → photos → availability → publish.

**Get more visibility (provider):**
> Promote your services → redeem points or buy directly → featured for 7 days.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Wasla?**
A mobile app that connects customers with trusted local service providers in Algeria — covering tailoring, sweets, cleaning, repairs, beauty, tutoring, events, and more.

**Do I need an account?**
Yes. Choose a role (customer or provider), register with your phone number, and verify it with a one-time code.

**Can I be both a customer and a provider?**
Your experience is based on the role you sign in with. Providers complete an extra store setup.

**How do I pay?**
Choose Cash, Gold Card, or BaridiMob at checkout. The booking screen shows a full price breakdown including any platform fee and discount.

**Is there a discount for new users?**
You can apply a promo code (such as a welcome code) on the booking details step to receive a discount.

**How do points work?**
You earn points for activity like bookings and reviews, progress through tiers, unlock badges, and redeem points for rewards — or, as a provider, to promote your store.

**How is my phone number protected?**
You communicate through in-app chat. Wasla advises against sharing your personal number before a booking is confirmed.

**What languages does Wasla support?**
Arabic (right-to-left), English, and French — switchable in settings.

**How do providers get paid?**
Earnings appear in the in-app wallet, where providers can view balance, top up, and withdraw.

**How do I get more bookings as a provider?**
Complete your store, add quality photos, keep services active, respond quickly in chat, and use **Promote** to feature your store for 7 days.

## Troubleshooting tips

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| **No services appear / "You seem to be offline"** | Check your internet connection and tap **Retry**. |
| **Location-based results look wrong** | Allow location access, or set your city manually from the location screen. |
| **"Couldn't access your location" on the map** | Enable location permission for Wasla in your device settings. |
| **Verification code didn't arrive** | Wait for the resend timer, then tap **Resend**. (In demo mode any six-digit code works.) |
| **Invalid phone number** | Enter a valid Algerian number — 9 digits after the +213 prefix. |
| **Promo code rejected** | Double-check the code; an invalid code shows an error. Try a valid welcome code. |
| **Can't change my phone number** | Phone numbers are changed via support — contact the Help Center. |
| **Can't upload a profile photo** | Photo upload is coming in a future update; other profile fields can be edited now. |
| **No bookings/chats/favorites yet** | These areas show a helpful empty state — place a booking, message a provider, or save a service to populate them. |
| **Need more help** | Open the **Help Center**, search articles, or use **Live Chat** / **Call** support. |
